How it works

Every /extension/src/**.html and /dev/demo*.html page gets /dev/chrome-mock.js and the real service-worker.js injected by scripts/dev-server.mjs; demo pages also get the manifest's content scripts. Storage lives in localStorage (shared by all tabs), the toolbar badge is shown in the small dev bar at the bottom-right, Alt+Shift+C fires the toggle-enabled command. Language / theme: ?lang=vi, ?theme=dark or the buttons above (they apply to every page, including the split-view frames).

The real popup, talking to the demo frame's content script (GET_PAGE_INFO, RESCAN) over the mock's bus.
