    $ and ¥

    Several currencies share these symbols. TransPrice guesses from the website first and uses your choice below only when the page gives no hint.

    $

    Canadian, Australian and other shops are recognised by their address.

    ¥

    Taobao, Tmall, 1688 and JD always use yuan; Japanese sites always use yen.
